Here we go again. 

When **WoW Forever** finally opened its doors for closed beta pre-purchase early access at 2:00 PM EST on a Thursday, optimism was already hanging by a thread for many in the community. But whatever goodwill Blizzard had managed to build up with their recent announcements was torched within the first sixty minutes. 

If you paid $60 or $80 for the pre-purchase tiers specifically to dive into early access, your reward was a front-row seat to an absolute, unmitigated clusterfuck.

---

## 1. The 30+ Minute Queue and Disconnect Loop

The immediate bottleneck hit right at the login screen. Players were instantly greeted by staggering queue times stretching past 30 minutes.

<GuideImage alt="World of Warcraft character select screen showing a persistent World Server Down error message" src="/images/blog/beta_login_queue_02.png" credit="Image: Screenshot courtesy of my Guildies"/>
    
* **The Disconnect Trap:** The real insult wasn't just waiting half an hour; it was managing to weather the queue only to experience a world server crash or random disconnect the second you loaded into the game world. 
* **Back to Square One:** Getting booted meant being thrust right back to the *end* of an even longer queue. Watching your precious early access hours tick away while staring at a static position number is a special kind of frustration.

## 2. History Repeated: The Blizzard Launch Special

What makes this sting the most is the sheer predictability of it. 

* **The Track Record:** We watched this exact same infrastructure failure unfold during the massive Classic WoW launch in 2019, and again during the anniversary releases in 2024. 
* **The Incompetence:** You would genuinely think that after two decades of running the biggest MMO on the planet—and repeatedly stumbling over the exact same traffic-scaling hurdles—Blizzard would over-provision capacity for a launch. But alas, it's the same old story. 

---

## 3. Punishing Your Most Dedicated (and Paying) Fans

This wasn't even an open, free-for-all launch. This was a **closed beta early access phase** gated behind hefty $60 and $80 pre-purchase price tags. 

* **Alienating the Fence-Sitters:** For players who were already on the fence about whether *WoW Forever* was worth investing time and money into, this launch day disaster answered their questions for them. 
* **A Slap in the Face:** Treating your most dedicated, paying supporters—the ones willing to shell out top dollar right out of the gate—to a subpar server experience leaves an immediately sour taste. Imagine how much worse this would have been if they tried dropping this at 6:00 PM EST on a Friday instead of a Thursday afternoon.
