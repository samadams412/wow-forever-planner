Culinary arts expand with a refreshing lineup of six unique smoothie recipes. While relying entirely on classic vanilla ingredients, each beverage provides restorative Spirit attribute boosts accompanied by the signature **Well Fed** experience bonus. Unlike primary manufacturing trades, however, the culinary vendor does not offer a professional certification title.
