For Cooking, we spotted 6 new recipes for smoothies, all of which grant varying amounts of Spirit, and all of which use materials that are present in vanilla WoW.

Like all food in WoW Forever, the **Well Fed** buff from these recipes gives an experience increase in addition to their stat bonuses.

Unlike the primary crafting professions, the Cooking vendor does not offer an item that grants a title.

## Smoothie Recipes

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/cooking/wow-forever-cooking-calcified-smoothie.webp" alt="A glowing, crystal-infused calcified smoothie in a sturdy glass mug" />

* **Calcified Smoothie:** Grants a boost to your Spirit and XP gain.
* **Mrrggl Smrrthle:** A specialized murloc-inspired blend favored by coastal travelers.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/cooking/wow-forever-cooking-slimy-smoothie.webp" alt="A thick, vibrant green slimy smoothie bubbling slightly with mysterious ingredients" />

* **Slimy Smoothie:** Surprisingly refreshing despite its unappealing name and texture.
* **Spicy Smoothie:** Packed with hot spices to kickstart your regeneration in the field.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/cooking/wow-forever-cooking-venomous-smoothie-1.webp" alt="A dark, menacing venomous smoothie garnished with strange exotic herbs" />

* **Venomous Smoothie:** Brewed carefully to harness medicinal properties without the poison.
* **Wicked Smoothie:** A potent concoction that leaves a chilling, long-lasting aftertaste.
