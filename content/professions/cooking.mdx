For Cooking, we spotted 6 new recipes for smoothies, all of which grant varying amounts of Spirit, and all of which use materials that are present in vanilla WoW.

Like all food in WoW Forever, the **Well Fed** buff from these recipes gives an experience increase in addition to their stat bonuses.

Unlike the primary crafting professions, the Cooking vendor does not offer an item that grants a title.
