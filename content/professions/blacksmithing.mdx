Blacksmithing expands its anvil offerings with an extensive catalog of mail and plate armor sets alongside high-impact craftable weapons and a professional certification title. While traditional vanilla metals form the primary framework, artisans must source novel components including Azerothium Bars, Legionite Bars, Torbernite, and Death Lotus.
