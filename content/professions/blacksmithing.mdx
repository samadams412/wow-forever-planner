There aren’t any new craftable item types for Blacksmithing, but there is plenty of new gear to be made and one item that unlocks a special title for the profession.

Most of the materials required are vanilla, but there are some new ones, including Azerothium Bar, Legionite Bar, Torbernite, and Death Lotus.

## Weapons

There are three new crafted weapons in the mix:

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/blacksmithing/wow-forever-blacksmithing-legionite-glaive.webp" alt="A jagged, dark green-tinted legionite glaive glowing with fel energy" />

* **Legionite Glaive:** A menacing blade forged from rare legionite materials.
* **Thorium Cestus:** Heavy-duty spiked hand-wraps built with reinforced thorium plating.
* **Thorium Greatmace:** A massive, crushing two-handed mace designed to shatter armor.

## Acolyte’s Set

There are six pieces of Acolyte’s mail armor that increase healing, spell damage, Intellect, and Stamina.

* **Acolyte's Boots & Gloves:** Basic mail protection focusing on mental clarity and spell output.
* **Acolyte's Chain Belt:** A sturdy mail waistguard woven for spellcasting fighters.
* **Acolyte’s Silvered Chain Helm, Shirt & Leggings:** Core silvered chain pieces providing robust magical enhancement.

## Crusader’s Set

The Crusader’s mail armor items increase spell damage and healing as well as Strength and Stamina.

* **Crusader’s Gloves, Boots & Chain Belt:** Flexible mail pieces tailored for hybrid combatants.
* **Crusader’s Silvered Chain Helm, Shirt & Leggings:** Heavy silvered chain forged to empower both physical strikes and spell power.

## Enriched Thorium Set

There are 3 pieces of Enchanted Thorium plate armor that increase Defense, spell damage and healing, and Stamina.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/blacksmithing/wow-forever-blacksmithing-enriched-thorium-breastplate.webp" alt="An ornate enriched thorium breastplate shimmering with protective magical runes" />

* **Enriched Thorium Helm:** A heavily reinforced plate helmet providing solid defensive wards.
* **Enriched Thorium Breastplate:** A glowing chestpiece built to withstand heavy blows while boosting magical capability.
* **Enriched Thorium Leggings:** Sturdy plate leg guards crafted for tanking hybrid specs.

## Guard’s Set

The Guard’s armor pieces are mail and provide Defense as well as Stamina and Strength (or Agility, on one of the pieces).

* **Guard’s Gloves, Boots & Chain Belt:** Reliable protective mail components for frontier guards.
* **Guard’s Silvered Chain Helm, Shirt & Leggings:** Full coverage mail armor geared heavily toward mitigation.

## Justicar’s Set

The Justicar armor pieces are plate and increase damage and healing as well as Strength and Stamina.

* **Justicar’s Pauldrons & Wristguards:** Imposing plate components built for holy crusaders.
* **Justicar’s Gauntlet, Waistguard & Sabatons:** Heavy plate pieces that balance offensive power with defensive stability.

## Officer’s Set

The Officer’s gear pieces are plate and provide only primary stats – Strength, Agility, and Stamina.

* **Officer’s Pauldrons & Wristguard:** Sleek plate armor pieces denoting high military rank.
* **Officer’s Gauntlet, Waistguard & Sabatons:** Pure stat-focused equipment for optimal physical output.

## Prefect’s Set

The Prefect’s gear items are plate and increase your healing, spell damage, Intellect, and Stamina.

* **Prefect’s Pauldrons & Wristguard:** Rare plate items customized for spell-wielding commanders.
* **Prefect’s Gauntlet, Waistguard & Sabatons:** Sturdy plate boots, gloves, and belt rounding out the caster-plate aesthetic.

## Protector’s Set

Protector’s items are mail and increase Defense, spell damage, healing, and Stamina.

* **Protector’s Silvered Chain Helm, Shirt & Leggings:** Mail pieces optimized for frontline tanking hybrids.
* **Protector’s Gloves & Chain Belt:** Flexible protective gear to round out defensive setups.
* **Protector’s Boots:** Heavy-treaded mail boots built for holding the line.

## Sentinel’s Set

The Sentinel’s items are plate gear that grants Defense, Strength, and Stamina.

* **Sentinel’s Wristguard & Gauntlet:** Arm protection forged for unyielding defense.
* **Sentinel’s Sabatons, Waistguard & Pauldrons:** Heavy plate pieces designed to absorb maximum impact.

## Veteran’s Set

Veteran’s gear items are mail and only increase primary stats: Strength, Agility, and Stamina.

* **Veteran’s Gloves, Boots & Chain Belt:** Essential mail accessories for seasoned combatants.
* **Veteran’s Silvered Chain Helm, Shirt & Leggings:** Core mail pieces offering clean, unadulterated physical stats.

## Warder’s Set

Items with the Warder’s prefix offer Defense, spell damage and healing, and Stamina.

* **Warder’s Pauldrons, Wristguard & Gauntlet:** Specialized plate gear designed for battlemage wardens.
* **Warder’s Waistguard & Sabatons:** Secure plate components providing extra resilience and magic-infusion.

## Profession Title

As with the other crafting professions, there is a certification item that unlocks a unique title if you have 300 skill in the profession.
