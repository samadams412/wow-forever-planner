For Enchanting, there were 40 new items in total, which included both enchants and new craftable items as well as an item that unlocks a title. Some of these require new materials that don’t exist in WoW Classic such as new dyes.

## Idols

Enchanting can be used to craft some interesting Idols for the Relic slot, only usable by Druids. There seems to be one for each specialization.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/enchanting/wow-forever-enchanting-idol-of-the-dream.webp" alt="An intricately carved wooden idol pulsing with a soft, natural dreamlike aura" />

* **Idol of the Dream:** A tranquil relic tuned to support restoration druids.
* **Magic Mushroom:** A whimsical yet powerful growth attuned to feral or balance capabilities.
* **Talons of Wrath:** An aggressive Relic crafted to sharpen offensive combat forms.

## Librams

Enchanting can also craft Librams for the Relic slot, which are exclusive to Paladins. 

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/enchanting/wow-forever-enchanting-libram-of-holy-alacrity.webp" alt="A bound holy libram glowing with a faint golden light across its ancient cover" />

* **Libram of Holy Alacrity:** Inscribed with sacred rites to speed up holy execution.
* **Libram of Invocation:** Channeled to enhance divine blessings and spell output.
* **Tenets of the Silver Hand:** A righteous tome outlining the strict codes of holy defense.

## Totems

Like the other Relic slot items, Enchanting can craft some Totems, which are only for Shamans.

* **Polished Driftwood Icon:** A weathered totem carved from ocean wood, channeling elemental protection.
* **Totem of Ancestral Protection:** Woven with spiritual energy to shield allies from harm.
* **Totem of Thunder:** A crackling conduit that amplifies storm and lightning capabilities.

## Wands

There is one new craftable Wand for the Ranged slot among the new recipes we saw.

* **Brilliant Wand:** A refined magical wand emitting a steady, focused beam of arcane energy.

## Staves

Enchanting can craft some Staff weapons, giving spell casters a way to craft their own weapons rather than needing to go to a Blacksmith.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/enchanting/wow-forever-enchanting-dreamstaff.webp" alt="A tall, elegant wooden dreamstaff topped with a glowing ethereal crystal" />

* **Dreamstaff:** A mystic two-hander imbued with deep, restful magic.
* **Glimmering Staff:** Shimmers with ambient enchantments, boosting raw spell power.
* **Radiant Staff:** A brightly burning wooden stave designed for aggressive spellcasting.
* **Soulstaff:** A dark, fascinating conduit channeling spiritual life-force.

## Off-Hands

There’s one more equippable item type among the Enchanting recipes, and those are Held In Off-hand items for the Off-Hand slot.

* **Orb of Mystic Insight:** A floating sphere that expands mental clarity and intellect.
* **Orb of Souls:** Pulses with trapped energy to empower shadow and arcane magic.
* **Truesilver Conduit:** A metallic off-hand focusing raw magical throughput.
* **Twisting Essence Jar:** Contains a swirling vortex of volatile elemental energy.

## Bracer Enchants

Starting off the enchantment formulas are Enchanting recipes for the Wrist slot.

* **Enchant Bracer - Greater Spellpower:** Permanently increases spell damage on bracers.
* **Enchant Bracer - Superior Deflection:** Fortifies wrist armor to increase defense ratings.
* **Enchant Bracer - Superior Intellect:** Substantially boosts the wearer's mental pool.

## Glove Enchants

There are also some enchants for the Hands slot. Three of these increase a specific type of elemental damage.

* **Enchant Gloves - Arcane Power:** Amplifies arcane spell effectiveness.
* **Enchant Gloves - Holy Power:** Increases the potency of holy spells.
* **Enchant Gloves - Natural Power:** Enhances nature-based damage output.
* **Enchant Gloves - Superior Strength:** Hardens grip and boosts raw physical strength.

## Chest Enchants

There’s one lone enchantment formula for the Chest slot. It provides a damage absorption proc.

* **Enchant Chest - Absorption:** Grants your chest armor a chance to absorb incoming damage on hit.

## Cloak Enchants

There’s one new cloak enchant formula that gives Agility.

* **Enchant Cloak - Agility:** Weaves lightweight agility enhancements into your cape.

## Neck Enchants

Neck items can’t be enchanted in any version of WoW Classic, but it looks like that’s going to change in Classic Forever with new formulas for this slot.

* **Enchant Necklace - Agility:** Adds nimble reflex bonuses to necklaces.
* **Enchant Necklace - Deflection:** Reinforces neck pieces with defensive wards.
* **Enchant Necklace - Healing Power:** Imbues pendants with restorative life energy.
* **Enchant Necklace - Spell Power:** Increases raw magical damage capability.
* **Enchant Necklace - Strength:** Bolsters physical might through an enchanted amulet.

## Off-Hand Enchants

Off-Hands couldn’t originally be enchanted until The Burning Crusade, but there is a formula for this slot in WoW Forever.

* **Enchant Off Hand - Wisdom:** Enhances off-hand items with steady mana regeneration properties.

## Weapon Enchants

Finally, there are several Enchanting recipes for weapons, including some that are only usable on two-handed weapons.

* **Enchant Weapon - Insight:** Grants a chance to restore mana when casting spells.
* **Enchant Weapon - Recovery:** Speeds up combat health and resource recovery.
* **Enchant Weapon - Revelation:** Pierces enemy resistances with mystical insight.
* **Enchant 2H Weapon - Mighty Healing Power:** Massively boosts healing capabilities on two-handers.
* **Enchant 2H Weapon - Spellblasting:** Tailored two-handed enchantment for offensive spellcasters.
* **Enchant 2H Weapon - Mighty Spell Power:** Grants a huge surge of raw spell power to large staves and weapons.

## Profession Title

Each crafting profession has its own certification item that unlocks a unique title. You can only use this title if you have 300 skill in the profession.
