For Alchemy, we collected the tooltips for 29 new recipes and an item that unlocks a title. All of these recipes use herbs and vials from the vanilla game.

## Thrown AoE Potions

There are two potions that are thrown at a targeted location to deal damage or debuff enemies.

## DoT Potions

Two of the new potions apply damage-over-time effects to enemies.

## Profession Title

Every crafting profession gets its own certification item that unlocks a special title, which is only usable if you have 300 skill in the profession.
