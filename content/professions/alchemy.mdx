For Alchemy, we collected the tooltips for 29 new recipes and an item that unlocks a title. All of these recipes use herbs and vials from the vanilla game.

## Thrown AoE Potions

There are two potions that are thrown at a targeted location to deal damage or debuff enemies.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/alchemy/wow-forever-alchemy-caustic-smog-potion.webp" alt="A green glass flask swirling with corrosive vapors ready to be thrown" />

* **Caustic Smog Potion:** Hurls a volatile chemical mixture that eats away at enemy armor in an area effect.
* **Disorienting Smog Potion:** Releases a blinding cloud that leaves targets wandering in confusion.

## DoT Potions

Two of the new potions apply damage-over-time effects to enemies.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/alchemy/wow-forever-alchemy-dragonfire-potion.webp" alt="A fiery red vial radiating intense heat with small ember sparks floating inside" />

* **Dragonfire Potion:** Coats the target in burning liquid fire that deals continuous fire damage over time.
* **Potion of Venomous Blood:** Infuses the target's veins with a toxic mixture, causing escalating nature damage.

## Stat Increase Potions

There are also potions that increase various stats for 30 seconds. One of them only works against elemental enemies.

* **Frenzy Potion & Major Frenzy Potion:** Briefly accelerates your attack speed and physical output.
* **Mender's Potion & Major Mender's Potion:** Temporarily boosts healing output for emergency situations.
* **Spellblasting Potion & Major Spellblasting Potion:** Enhances spellcasting haste and damage for a short burst window.
* **Potion of Elemental Siphoning:** Draws power from surrounding elements, granting massive combat enhancements specifically against elemental enemies.

## Health & Mana Elixirs

Some of the elixir recipes increase health and mana regeneration for 30 minutes.

* **Elixir of Greater Fortitude:** Substantially raises total health pool and general stamina endurance.
* **Elixir of the Phalanx:** Fortifies defenses, bolstering armor and block capabilities.
* **Elixir of Wicked Regeneration:** Accelerates natural health recovery significantly in and out of combat.
* **Lesser Mageblood Elixir & Greater Mageblood Elixir:** Restores mana steadily over a long duration, perfect for extended spellcasting sessions.

## Healing & Spell Damage Elixirs

A few elixirs increase healing or spell damage for 30 minutes. One of them is specifically for Nature damage.

* **Lesser Cleric's Elixir & Greater Cleric's Elixir:** Amplifies all healing spells cast by the consumer.
* **Lesser Arcane Elixir:** Boosts raw magical potency across all arcane and general spell schools.
* **Elixir of Nature Power:** Specifically enhances the potency of Nature-based damage and spells.

## Stat Increase Elixirs

Finally, there are some straightforward stat increasing elixirs that also last for 30 minutes.

* **Elixir of Cunning:** Increases agility and tactical combat awareness.
* **Elixir of Ferocity:** Enhances raw physical strength and hitting power.
* **Elixir of Lesser Spirit & Elixir of Greater Spirit:** Boosts spirit regeneration rates.
* **Elixir of the Grizzly:** Hardens the body, raising stamina and physical mitigation.
* **Elixir of Lesser Intellect:** Expands total mana pool and mental acuity.
* **Elixir of the Owl:** Focuses intellect and magical precision.
* **Elixir of Sages:** Combines intelligence and spirit upgrades into a single elixir.
* **Elixir of the Whale:** Grants a balanced boost to both stamina and spirit.

## Profession Title

Every crafting profession gets its own certification item that unlocks a special title, which is only usable if you have 300 skill in the profession.
