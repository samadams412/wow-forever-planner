Alchemy expands its transmutative catalog with 29 new recipes alongside an exclusive professional title certification. Utilizing vanilla herbs and glass vials exclusively, these formulations introduce thrown area-of-effect utilities, damage-over-time applications, short-term burst enhancement potions, and long-duration attribute elixirs.
