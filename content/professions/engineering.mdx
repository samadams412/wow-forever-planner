For Engineering, there are some very interesting new schematics on offer. These recipes mostly use vanilla crafting materials, but there are a few new ones in the mix, including Azerothium Bar, Legionite Bar, Torbernite, Marefoil, and Viridian Dye. There’s also an item that unlocks a new title for each profession.

It’s worth noting that most of these items are currently BoE and don’t require Engineering to use. So even if some of them are very powerful, you won’t necessarily be obligated to level the profession in order to have access to them.

## Head

Goggles with unique effects have always been one of the perks of Engineering, and there is a new set of them available at this vendor for each armor type which have a use effect that works like the Ornate Spyglass, allowing you to see further into the distance.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/engineering/wow-forever-engineering-bent-goggles.webp" alt="A pair of slightly scratched brass engineering goggles with multi-lenses" />

* **Bent Goggles:** Sturdy starter goggles fitted with distance-viewing lenses.
* **Dented Goggles:** Reinforced headgear built to withstand rough field conditions.
* **Floppy Goggles:** Flexible, lightweight mechanical goggles with adjustable view scopes.
* **Stuckbutton Goggles:** Quaint headgear featuring a finicky adjustment mechanism.

## Back

There is one item for the back slot that functions like the Parachute Cloak from Classic and seems to require it as a material, but which has charges. Its charges can be restored with the Ultrasafe Rechargeable Battery we show in the Miscellaneous section below. This is an odd one given that it seems to have more limitations than the Parachute Cloak it requires, but it’s likely that the Parachute Cloak has been changed in some way as well.

* **Saf't Disposable Parachute:** A charged cape attachment designed to slow your descent in emergencies.

## Waist

There are three items for the Waist slot that don’t seem to have any particular effects at the moment, so perhaps they’re not fully implemented yet.

* **Clanking Cord:** A heavy metal belt clinking with uncalibrated mechanical parts.
* **Gizmo Girdle:** A snug waistpiece fitted with empty gear slots and toggles.
* **Whimsical Waistwrap:** An eccentric belt featuring unpredictable gadgetry.

## Trinket

There are trinkets in the mix, including a powerful one that grants invisibility for 10 seconds. Like the parachute cloak above, this item has charges that can be restored with the Ultrasafe Rechargeable Battery. The other trinket is the Gnomish Poultryizer, an item that was originally added in The Burning Crusade.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/engineering/wow-forever-engineering-gnomish-poultryizer.webp" alt="A mechanical gnomish contraption shaped like a metal chicken with glowing red optic sensors" />

* **Emergency Field Cloak:** A sophisticated stealth device granting complete invisibility for a short window.
* **Gnomish Poultryizer:** A classic ray-gun trinket that transforms hostile targets into helpless chickens.

## Tinker

Tinkers are item enhancements that are applied like enchantments, and were originally added in Wrath of the Lich King. It looks like they’ll be available in WoW Forever, including one that gives an ability similar to a Mage’s Blink spell and the famously useful Nitro Boots that give a burst of speed on demand.

* **Tinker: Nitro Boots:** Attaches high-velocity thrusters to your footwear for a massive speed burst.
* **Tinker: Teleport:** Imbues gear with a spatial displacement drive for short-range blinking.
* **Tinkerbox:** A specialized tool kit containing components for advanced field enhancements.

## Not Yet Implemented

There are two items marked as “NYI,” which don’t have any effects listed. We can only guess at what the Interdimensional Companion Repository and Gnomish Weather Machine might be!

* **Interdimensional Companion Repository:** A mysterious device presumably designed to store or summon extra companions.
* **Gnomish Weather Machine:** An experimental atmospheric modifier with unknown meteorological effects.

## Miscellaneous

There are a few interesting new gadgets for Engineering. The Stealthman 52 has some potentially huge applications, allowing any class to access out of combat stealth on a cooldown. The Ultralight Goblin Glider gives a Slow Fall effect with a bonus – you can control the direction in which you move as you’re gliding. There’s also an item that can be used to restore charges to some of your other Engineering items that have limited uses.

* **Stealthman 52:** A portable stealth field generator usable by any class out of combat.
* **Ultralight Goblin Glider:** Deploys a steerable wing apparatus for controlled horizontal gliding and safe landings.
* **Ultrasafe Rechargeable Battery:** A reusable power source used to replenish charges on depleted engineering devices.

## Title

Each crafting profession has a certification item that unlocks a unique title, which is only usable if you have 300 skill in the profession.
