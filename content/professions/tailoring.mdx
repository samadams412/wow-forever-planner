There weren’t any new types of craftable items for Tailoring, but there were plenty of pieces of cloth gear and a couple of bags. There was also an item that unlocks a special title.

Most of the crafting materials used are from the vanilla game, but there are some new types of dye mentioned: Magenta Dye, Cerulean Dye, and Viridian Dye. All of these items are BoE once crafted and can be used by non-tailors.

## Bags

There are two new bags among the patterns, both reagent bags. We covered the addition of reagent bags in WoW Forever in our Changes for Bags & Banks article.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/tailoring/wow-forever-tailoring-runecloth-reagent-bag.webp" alt="A finely stitched runecloth bag glowing faintly with mystical runes for holding crafting materials" />

* **Mageweave Reagent Bag:** A handy medium-sized container dedicated specifically to storing crafting reagents.
* **Runecloth Reagent Bag:** A spacious, high-capacity pouch designed to keep a crafter's inventory organized.

## Black Set

These items increase Shadow damage along with Intellect and Stamina.

* **Black Handwraps, Sandals & Waistcord:** Dark cloth accessories tailored for aspiring shadow weavers.

## Fiery Set

These items increase Fire damage, Intellect, and Stamina.

* **Fiery Waistcord, Handwraps & Sandals:** Warm, ember-threaded cloth pieces crafted to maximize fire spell output.

## Flame Set

This is another batch of gear that increases Fire damage, Intellect, and Stamina.

<GuideImage src="/images/professions/tailoring/wow-forever-tailoring-flame-gown.webp" alt="An ornate orange and red cloth gown radiating a shimmering thermal heat wave" />

* **Flame Circlet, Gown & Leggings:** Core pieces providing substantial fire damage enhancements.
* **Flame Sash, Boots & Gloves:** Supporting slot items to complete the full pyromancer ensemble.

## Frothing Set

This set increases Frost damage along with Intellect and Stamina.

* **Frothing Waistcord, Handwraps & Sandals:** Chilly, ice-infused cloth components built for frost spellcasters.

## Gilded Set

This set increases healing done, Intellect, and Stamina, plus spell damage to a lesser degree.

* **Gilded Waistcord & Sandals:** Elegant golden-trimmed pieces designed for holy and restorative spellcasters.

## Golden Set

These items increase Arcane damage along with Intellect and Stamina.

* **Golden Waistcord, Handwraps & Sandals:** Shimmering yellow cloth gear tailored to boost arcane power.

## Netherflame Set

These items increase Fire damage, Intellect, Stamina, and Spirit.

* **Netherflame Shoulders & Cuffs:** Advanced outland-grade cloth armor amplifying volatile fire magic.

## Netherfroth Set

These items increase Frost damage along with Intellect, Stamina, and Spirit.

* **Netherfroth Shoulders & Cuffs:** Premium specialized gear for frost-oriented spell slingers.

## Nethergeld Set

This set increases healing done plus Intellect, Spirit, and Stamina, and spell damage to a lesser degree.

* **Nethergeld Shoulders & Cuffs:** High-end restorative cloth armor designed for dedicated healers.

## Netherlight Set

These items increase Holy damage plus Intellect, Spirit, and Stamina.

* **Netherlight Shoulders & Cuffs:** Radiant cloth accessories channeling divine energy.

## Netherpearl Set

This set increases Shadow damage plus Intellect, Spirit, and Stamina.

* **Netherpearl Shoulders & Cuffs:** Dark, pearl-accented cloth pieces boosting shadow potency.

## Nethershine Set

These items improve Arcane damage, Intellect, Spirit, and Stamina.

* **Nethershine Shoulders & Cuffs:** Glowing arcane mantle and wrist wraps for advanced magi.

## Pearly Set

Another set that increases Arcane damage plus Intellect and Stamina.

* **Pearly Circlet, Gown & Leggings:** Lustrous white garments optimized for arcane spellcasters.
* **Pearly Sash & Boots:** Matching footwear and belt accessories to complement the set.

## Pristine Set

These items increase healing as well as a smaller amount of spell damage plus Intellect and Stamina.

* **Pristine Circlet, Gown & Leggings:** Impeccable cloth robes tailored for battlefield medics.
* **Pristine Sash, Boots & Gloves:** Clean white accessories bolstering restorative capabilities.

## Radiant Set

These items increase Holy damage plus Intellect and Stamina.

* **Radiant Waistcord, Handwraps & Sandals:** Brightly woven cloth pieces attuned to holy magic.

## Shadow Set

This set increases your Shadow damage plus Intellect and Stamina.

* **Shadow Circlet, Gown & Leggings:** Dark, ominous robes built for practitioners of shadow arts.
* **Shadow Sash, Boots & Gloves:** Complementary dark accessories to round out the set.

## Shining Set

These items increase Holy damage, Intellect, and Stamina.

* **Shining Circlet, Gown & Leggings:** Luminescent holy vestments designed to empower divine spells.
* **Shining Sash, Boots & Gloves:** Polished white and gold support pieces.

## Silky Set

These increase Frost damage, Intellect, and Stamina.

* **Silky Circlet, Gown & Leggings:** Smooth, cold-resistant garments tailored for frost mages.
* **Silky Sash, Boots & Gloves:** Soft silk accessories enhancing ice-based combat.

## Title

Every crafting profession gets a certification item that unlocks a unique title which requires 300 skill in the profession to use.
