There weren’t any new types of craftable items for Tailoring, but there were plenty of pieces of cloth gear and a couple of bags. There was also an item that unlocks a special title.

Most of the crafting materials used are from the vanilla game, but there are some new types of dye mentioned: Magenta Dye, Cerulean Dye, and Viridian Dye. All of these items are BoE once crafted and can be used by non-tailors.

## Bags

There are two new bags among the patterns, both reagent bags. We covered the addition of reagent bags in WoW Forever in our Changes for Bags & Banks article.
