While Tailoring does not introduce entirely new categories of manufactured items, it expands significantly with a diverse catalog of elemental cloth garments and specialized storage pouches. Furthermore, mastering the trade provides access to an exclusive profession title.

Most patterns rely on classic foundational materials supplemented by novel chromatic inputs, specifically Magenta Dye, Cerulean Dye, and Viridian Dye. Like many gathering and crafting additions in this expansion, these completed garments are Bind-on-Equip (BoE), allowing non-tailors to fully benefit from them.

## Bags

Tailors gain access to two specialized storage containers geared toward material management, expanding inventory utility for gathering professions as detailed in our comprehensive bag and bank overview.
