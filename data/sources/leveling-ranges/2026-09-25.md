Alliance
Human: Elwynn Forest (1–10) → Westfall (10–20), with Loch Modan as an alternative

Night Elf: Teldrassil (1–10) → Darkshore (10–20)
Skyborne (Alliance): Zephras Isle (1–12). Then most likely through Darkshore, Westfall, or Loch Modan to finish the 12–20 stretch.
